# Andrej Karpathy: fact ledger

The machine-checkable half of the research record. `sources.md` is the prose a human reads; this
file is what `scripts/fact-ledger.ts` reads. One line per verified fact:

    - [S:n] claim | url | date checked

Every factual field on the page cites these IDs from `page-claims.md`. A claim with no line here
cannot reach the page, whoever proposed it, including a Council seat.

Written during the stage 1 research pass, 2026-09-22, for candidate issue #25. Each line records
what **in the document** supports the claim, and quotes the passage wherever the claim rests on the
specific wording. The Beck round-3 park happened because a line recorded only that a post credited
no antecedent while the field citing it needed what the post actually said, so the wording is
carried here rather than summarised.

- [S:31] What an adversarial example is, which the `extension`'s closing clause turns on and which round 1 seat 4 asserted without a source. Goodfellow, Shlens and Szegedy, "Explaining and Harnessing Adversarial Examples," submitted 2014-12-20. The abstract's own definition, quoted: machine learning models including neural networks "consistently misclassify adversarial examples---inputs formed by applying small but intentionally worst-case perturbations to examples from the dataset, such that the perturbed input results in the model outputting an incorrect answer with high confidence." What this line supports and what it does not: it supports that a model scoring well on the data it was evaluated against still misclassifies intentionally worst-case inputs with high confidence, which is what licenses the page's clause that a criterion passed on a held-out set says nothing about attacker-chosen inputs.
