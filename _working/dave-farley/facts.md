## The deployment pipeline: who, when, and what the record says

- [S:18] Farley, "The Deployment Pipeline (Extending the range of Continuous Integration)," 2007, © ThoughtWorks, Inc. – ThoughtWorks Studios, 11 pages, single author. It says the teams he worked on had been building these systems "for the past couple of years," and credits no originator. Footnote: "We want the same binary to be run-able in any environment." ## Critics

- [S:27] Mark Seemann, "Are pull requests bad because they originate from open-source development?," blog.ploeh.dk, 2023-04-24. Durable artifact (an essay on his own blog). The quoted words are Seemann's transcription of the video, not Farley's text; Seemann says to watch the video to be sure.
