# David Holmgren: fact ledger

Started 2026-10-18 at the uptake check (stage 1, first step). Bill Mollison is recorded as co-originator of permaculture; Andrew Millison is tested as a possible originator of frames.

- [S:1] Holmgren, "Essence of Permaculture" (revised 2020, his site), states: "The word permaculture was coined by Bill Mollison and myself in the mid-1970's to describe an integrated, evolving system of perennial or self-perpetuating plant and animal species useful to man." It gives a "more current definition ... which reflects the expansion of focus implicit in Permaculture One." The text is marked "Adapted from Introduction: Permaculture Principles & Pathways Beyond Sustainability. Copyright © 2002." | https://holmgren.com.au/downloads/Essence_of_Pc_EN.pdf | 2026-10-18
- [S:2] The same document [S:1] lists the twelve design principles: 1 Observe and interact; 2 Catch and store energy; 3 Obtain a yield; 4 Apply self-regulation and accept feedback; 5 Use and value renewable resources and services; 6 Produce no waste; 7 Design from patterns to details; 8 Integrate rather than segregate; 9 Use small and slow solutions; 10 Use and value diversity; 11 Use edges and value the marginal; 12 Creatively use and respond to change. It says "I organise the diversity of permaculture thinking under 12 design principles," and "Like design principles, the ethics were not explicitly listed in early permaculture literature," so the twelve-principle set is Holmgren's own organisation, published 2002. | https://holmgren.com.au/downloads/Essence_of_Pc_EN.pdf | 2026-10-18
- [S:3] Antecedent named by Holmgren himself [S:1]: he acknowledges "clear and special debt to the published work of American ecologist Howard Odum," says Odum's influence "is made explicit in the dedication and extensive references to Odum in Permaculture, Principles & Pathways Beyond Sustainability," and adds "Apart from the ecological energetics of Howard Odum, the influence of systems thinking in my" work. | https://holmgren.com.au/downloads/Essence_of_Pc_EN.pdf | 2026-10-18
- [S:4] Energy descent, in the same document [S:1]: "I call this the 'energy descent future' to emphasise the primacy of energy", and "Permaculture uses these conditions to consciously and continuously evolve systems of land use and living that can sustain people through the era of energy descent." The document places the frame by 2002 (adapted from the book introduction) and in its 2020 revision; the first dated use was not established in this pass. | https://holmgren.com.au/downloads/Essence_of_Pc_EN.pdf | 2026-10-18
- [S:5] Mollison is credited in the same document [S:1]: "The original permaculture vision, promoted by Bill Mollison, of urban landscapes full of food and other useful plants rather than useless ornamentals," and "Bill Mollison defined a pollutant as 'an output of any system component that is not being used productively by any other component of the system.'" | https://holmgren.com.au/downloads/Essence_of_Pc_EN.pdf | 2026-10-18

- [S:9] Outside use, computing community: the Permacomputing wiki describes permacomputing as "a concept and a community of practice oriented around issues of resilience and regenerativity in computer and network technology inspired by permaculture." An older principles file says the principles "have been modeled after those of permaculture." No Holmgren name was found on the pages read. | https://permacomputing.net/principles/ | 2026-10-18
