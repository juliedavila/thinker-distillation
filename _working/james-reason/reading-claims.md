# Reading claims: James Reason

One line per sentence of `reading` and `extension`, each with the grounding-pack IDs it rests on.

## reading

- Reason separates active failures, the unsafe acts at a system's front line, from latent conditions, the "resident pathogens" left in place by earlier decisions. [F:latent-conditions] [T:thesis]
- His co-authored history calls the observation behind the pair "nothing new", crediting Barry Turner and Charles Perrow. [F:latent-conditions] [T:thesis]
- He and John Wreathall drew the account as five planes, which later became slices of Swiss cheese, a label someone else supplied; an accident needs their holes to align. [F:swiss-cheese-model]
- In 1997 latent failures became latent conditions, because "a condition is not a cause, but it is necessary for a causal factor to have an impact." [F:latent-conditions]
- He argued that an organisation learns of its hazards through its people's reports, and that reporting rests on a just culture that separates acceptable from unacceptable behaviour. [F:just-culture] [T:thesis]
- He questioned the model's use, writing in 1997 that the search for distant contributions may have gone too far and asking in a 2004 talk whether it was past its sell-by date. [L:1]

## extension

- Defence in depth around an AI agent is a stack of partial controls, the arrangement Reason drew as slices. [F:swiss-cheese-model]
- His model was built to explain accidents, which nobody intends. [L:4]
- An injected instruction is written to find the alignment, inside the text every filtering layer reads. [L:4] [P:1]
- So those layers can fail together, and counting them overstates the protection. [L:4]
- The steadier lever is on the latent side: what the agent could reach before any text arrived. [F:latent-conditions]
- A standing permission, the authority a confused deputy needs, works like his resident pathogen, and the attacker supplies the trigger his account leaves to front-line acts. [F:latent-conditions] [T:thesis] [P:7]
- Removing it narrows what any injection can do, whichever layer it slipped past. [F:latent-conditions] [P:3]
- That fix carries a hazard close to the one his critics named: once a review finds the permission, it has a latent condition to blame, and the crafted text can drop out of the account. [L:2]
