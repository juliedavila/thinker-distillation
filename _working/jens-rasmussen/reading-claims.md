# Reading claims: Jens Rasmussen

One line per sentence of `reading` and `extension`, each with the grounding-pack IDs it rests on.

## reading

- Rasmussen gives one account of how work moves before an accident and another of how the analysis stops after one. [T:thesis] [F:migration-toward-the-boundary] [F:stop-rules]
- Workers follow an effort gradient, management supplies a cost gradient, and together they very likely produce a systematic migration toward the boundary of acceptable performance. [F:migration-toward-the-boundary]
- Under that pressure defences in depth are likely to degenerate over time, and in his account the accidents he studied were caused by the migration, with a coincidence of independent failures and human errors ruled out. [T:still-load-bearing] [T:thesis]
- He aims improvement at the boundary and away from errors: make it visible to the people who work near it, and control performance where work meets it. [T:working-line] [T:thesis]
- The analysis afterwards typically ends by stop rules that are rarely stated: the causal trail runs out, a familiar abnormal event explains enough, or a cure is available. [F:stop-rules]
- Which rule ends it depends on whether the analysis means to explain the events, to allocate responsibility and blame or to improve the system. [F:stop-rules]

## extension

- A security review stops by the same rarely stated rules, and on an attack surface whose exposures multiply one another, his third becomes dangerous: once a cure such as an unapplied patch appears, the review can end. [F:stop-rules] [P:5]
- The patch closes one path through the combination and leaves the others open. [P:5]
- An attacker can trip the second rule on purpose by planting a familiar explanation, such as a known exploit. [F:stop-rules]
- Writing down, before the review starts, its aim, its stop rule and the evidence that would rule out a familiar explanation makes the rules checkable. [F:stop-rules] [T:still-load-bearing]
- After a breach, advice on preserving privilege can put the forensic work under counsel, aimed at "litigation preparation" and kept "need-to-know", so a review meant to improve the system can lose the analyses it needs and stop at the first rule. [F:stop-rules]
- Writing the rules down costs time his cost gradient will press the team to give back. [F:migration-toward-the-boundary]
