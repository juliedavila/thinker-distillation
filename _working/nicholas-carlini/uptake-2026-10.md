## Antecedents

- Adversarial examples are Szegedy et al., 2013 [S:2], with Goodfellow among the authors. Carlini's contribution is the evaluation stance, that defenses fail against stronger attacks [S:1, S:3, S:9].
- Obfuscated gradients is named as "a kind of gradient masking" [S:3], so gradient masking is the prior term. Its originators were not dated in this pass.
- Membership inference (Shokri et al., 2017) and model inversion (Fredrikson et al., 2015) precede the extraction work. Neither was read in this pass, so these are leads, not ledger lines.
- Data poisoning as a class predates 2023 (Biggio et al. on SVMs, around 2012, not read). What is his is the web-scale, split-view mechanism [S:6].
