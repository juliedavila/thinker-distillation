## Named frames: antecedent searches

- [S:17] The antecedent search for "Principles" (numbered, written-down decision rules a person or organization commits to and revises; the page's gloss says "The genre is the contribution"): searched for earlier numbered personal and organizational principle sets and dated Dalio's own document.
