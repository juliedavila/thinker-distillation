# Reading claims: Ray Dalio

Protocol v1. One line per sentence of `reading` and `extension`, each with the grounding-pack IDs it rests on.

## reading

- Dalio argues that most consequential mistakes come from decision rules that were tacit, contradictory, or never examined. [T:thesis]
- His remedy is the principles document: numbered, written-down rules that anyone may challenge and that get revised when they produce bad outcomes. [F:principles] [T:thesis]
- Radical transparency and believability-weighted decisions support the document, the first by exposing rationales and the second by weighting each person's input by their track record on that kind of question. [F:radical-transparency] [F:believability-weighted-decision-making]
- The personal version is pain plus reflection equals progress. [F:pain-plus-reflection-equals-progress] [T:working-line]
- Reporting on Bridgewater describes a daily experience far harsher than the document's ideal, which makes the form and its implementation worth judging separately. [L:1] [T:still-load-bearing]

## extension

- Believability weighting struggles in security, because the best security calls leave no trace: a breach that never happened builds nobody's track record. [L:2]
- The principles move still transfers when it attaches to decisions instead of people, although what transfers is the written rule, and the track-record weighting that made the frame distinctive stays behind. [F:principles] [F:believability-weighted-decision-making] [L:2]
- A security organization can write its standing positions as numbered principles, each with its reasoning and the evidence that would change it. [F:principles] [P:1]
- Treating prompt injection as architecturally unsolvable, and therefore something to mitigate, is a principle of this kind: stated plainly, open to challenge, and revisable if the architecture changes. [P:1] [F:principles]
- Taleb's objection lands hard here, since a written principle says nothing about the attack nobody has written a principle for yet. [E:out:nassim-taleb]
- The other risk is the compliance theater the page already names, where the document survives audits and stops steering decisions. [L:3]
